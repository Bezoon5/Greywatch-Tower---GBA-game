# Changelog

## v6.0 (16 November 2026): first public release

- **The Fall** is rebuilt as a slide. Róisín slides down the back of the falling wyvern and steers against its tilt. When it lurches hard enough to throw her off, she drives her daggers in: mash B to climb back up. Jump clear at the very end, before it hits the moor.
- **The Burning Village** flashback is a third shorter.
- Fixes for split screens and garbage lines when the game is busy. The tutorial's ground is no longer too dark to see on GBA screens and emulators.

## Earlier development builds (v0.1 – v5.9)

- **Seven chapters:** The Wall, The Drowned Cathedral, The Midnight Bell Falls, The Ossuary, The Clockworks, The Black Dragon and The Last Midnight.
- **Two flashbacks:** The Burning Village and The Shackling.
- **Special stages:** the tutorial (Vey's Lessons), the confession walk, the bolt-thrower and the fall.
- **Systems:** Story Mode and Free Play; the bestiary; twelve hidden gears and a secret ending; the cheats page; the original soundtrack.
