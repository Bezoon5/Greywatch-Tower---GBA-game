The story

For a hundred years the great bell of Greywatch has tolled at midnight to keep the dead in their graves. Tonight a black dragon tears it down in a storm of fire, and the dead rise.

Two strangers hold the walls: Róisín, a storm-calling dancer with lightning daggers, and Aldaire, the last clockmaker of a vanished guild, who can stop time. Beneath the city, the bell has been holding back something far older than the dead.

Saving

The game saves your best score, settings, gears and bestiary to its cartridge save. In the browser that save is kept by your browser on this device. You can also use the player's save-state buttons.
